Thrust-vectoring physics

Engine thrust follows a first-order spool: dT/dt = (T_cmd − T) / τ. The nozzle swings about a spherical pivot. The exhaust direction and engine reaction force are illustrative.

Model axes: forward = −X, up = +Y, right = −Z. Pitch and yaw commands drive the simulated nozzle and actuators.
