Thrust-vectoring physics

Engine thrust follows a first-order spool: dT/dt = (T_cmd − T) / τ. The nozzle swings about a spherical pivot; the exhaust leaves along d = (cos p·cos y, −sin p, cos p·sin y) and the force on the engine is F = −T·d.

Axes: forward = −X, up = +Y, right = −Z. Pitch + → force up, yaw + → force right. Three actuators set the pose; their extensions come from the real linkage geometry.
