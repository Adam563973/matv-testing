MODEL NOTES & LIMITATIONS

The simulator integrates a fixed-step actuator model, a first-order asymmetric engine spool response, angle/rate saturation, estimated thrust components, thermal lag, and simplified nozzle area behaviour.

Audio is generated locally using Web Audio oscillators and filtered noise. It is an effect, not a recording. Visual reflections and glow approximate advanced rendering; no hardware ray tracing is used.

This is an educational visualisation. It is not an F-16 flight model, maintenance tool, engine performance map, or certified engineering simulation.
