MODEL NOTES & LIMITATIONS

The bench integrates a fixed-step rate-limited actuator model, asymmetric engine spool response, nozzle saturation, thermal lag, and estimated thrust-vector components. The flight scene uses simplified dynamic pressure, drag, lift, control moments, inertia, atmospheric density, and rate damping.

Aircraft silhouettes and aerodynamic coefficients are purpose-built approximations, not source-authentic aircraft models. The Cobra-style sequence is an illustrative high-angle-of-attack response demonstration, not a validated Pugachev's Cobra flight model.

Audio is synthesized locally and spatialised with Web Audio where supported. Shock diamonds, exhaust bloom, heat shimmer and airflow are real-time visual approximations; there is no CFD, hardware ray tracing, or certified F-16 performance data.
